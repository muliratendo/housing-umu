# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, the maintainers and contributors of **Housing@UMU — Student Accommodation Management Platform**,
pledge to make participation in this project and our community a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We are committed to creating a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all.

---

## Our standards

**Positive behaviour includes:**

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of different viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project.
- Showing empathy and kindness toward other community members.
- Giving credit where it is due and acknowledging others' work.

**Unacceptable behaviour includes:**

- Use of sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or educational setting.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions, documentation, and related communication channels), and in public spaces when
an individual is representing the project.

---

## Enforcement

Report instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to the project team:

- **Email:** `muliratendo@stud.umu.ac.ug`

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The project team will
maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter to the extent possible and appropriate.

**Enforcement actions (in order of severity):**

1. **Private feedback** — explain why the behaviour was inappropriate; request it stop.
2. **Public warning** — note the misconduct in the relevant channel.
3. **Temporary restriction** — limit participation in specific areas or the project as a whole.
4. **Permanent ban** — permanently remove the individual from the project.
